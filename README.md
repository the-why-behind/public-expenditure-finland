# Structure of Public Expenditure in Finland 1990–2024

This project examines what the Finnish public sector spends its money on and how the distribution of expenditure across functions changed over 1990–2024. Expenditure is measured as a share of GDP so that different years are comparable.

**Main question:** How has the distribution of public expenditure across functions changed, and which items have grown the most relative to GDP?

## Key findings
- Total public expenditure rose from 47.9% of GDP (1990) to about 57.7% (2024), with clear spikes during economic crises (early 1990s recession, 2009 financial crisis, 2020 pandemic).
- Almost all of the growth came from social protection (+7.8 percentage points), followed by health (+1.8) and general public services (+1.6).
- Spending on economic affairs fell the most (−1.5 percentage points).
